Payment methods and rates

The trigger name is what your hardware sends as payment_method to POST /grant. With exactly one method configured the device may omit it; with several it must say which one fired.

Quantity 1 is the base rate. Add higher quantities to reward bulk — 1 bottle for 30 min and 3 bottles for 2 hr means someone inserting 4 gets 2.5 hr, not 2 hr. Customers are always given the best-priced combination their quantity qualifies for.

Turn timeout

When it runs out the turn passes to the next person in the queue. Without a limit, someone who walks away blocks everybody behind them and the next payment anybody makes is credited to them instead.

Session cap

Customer portal wording

What customers read on the portal. Set these to match your actual hardware — a bottle chute, a coin slot and a card reader need different instructions. Leave a field empty to use the default.

Portal logo

Shown to customers on the captive portal. Uploads are resized and have their white background removed automatically, so a logo exported on a white card still sits cleanly on the portal. PNG or JPEG, up to 12 MB.

Recent transactions

Backup & restore

Restoring replaces every session, transaction and voucher with the contents of the uploaded file. The current database is saved alongside it first, the firewall is re-synced afterwards, and your admin password is kept as it is now (not taken from the backup).

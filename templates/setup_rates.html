What is one payment worth?

Nearly there. Before the machine can grant any time, tell it what your payment hardware sends and how much WiFi time that earns.

This ships with no default rate on purpose. The gateway does not know whether your machine takes bottles, coins, tokens or cards — and a guessed rate would quietly hand out time at a price you never chose.

The name is what your hardware sends as payment_method to POST /grant. Examples: bottle, coin, coin_5php, token, card.
